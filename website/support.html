Support

How can we help?

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Write to us — a real person replies.

The timer isn’t showing in the Dynamic Island or on the Lock Screen

Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Inly and make sure Live Activities is turned on. Then take your aligners out in Inly — the Live Activity starts as soon as a break begins. Live Activities need an iPhone with iOS 26 or later; the Dynamic Island needs an iPhone that has one.

I forgot to log a break. Can I add it later?

Yes. Open History, tap the day, then tap Add a missed break. You can also edit or delete any break from the same screen.

How do I change my daily wear goal?

Go to Settings → Treatment and choose the number of hours your orthodontist asked for. Your out-time budget, ring and reminders update right away.

I switched trays early or late. What should I do?

Just start the next tray when you actually switch. On the Trays screen, tap Start tray and choose today or yesterday. Inly saves how many days you spent on the previous tray and updates your estimated finish date.

My orthodontist ordered a refinement. How do I add it?

On the Trays screen, tap More → Start refinement and enter the number of new trays. Your earlier trays stay in your history with their own color, and the new trays start again from 1.

I finished my treatment and now wear a retainer

On the Trays screen, tap More → Finish treatment · start retainer. Choose full-time, every night or a few nights a week, and set a bedtime reminder if you like.

The Apple Watch app isn’t up to date

Open Inly on your iPhone once, and keep your iPhone nearby with Bluetooth on. The watch syncs directly with your iPhone; it can take a few moments after a change.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

With Inly+, turn on iCloud sync in Settings → Backup and data: on the new iPhone, Inly offers to restore your data on the first screen. You can also make a backup file on the old iPhone (same screen) and choose Restore from backup on the new one.

What is Inly+ and how much is it?

Inly+ is a one-time purchase that unlocks the Apple Watch app, widgets, iCloud sync, retainer mode, full history, unlimited photos and reports. It is not a subscription. The price is shown in the app before you buy. You can try it free for 7 days — the trial simply ends, with no automatic charge.

I bought Inly+ but it is locked on my new iPhone

Open Settings → Inly+ (or the Inly+ screen) and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it. Family members get Inly+ automatically through Family Sharing.

How does iCloud sync work?

Turn it on in Settings → Backup and data. Your history, trays, notes and settings are kept in your own iCloud and come back on a new iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID. Photos stay on the iPhone where you took them; use automatic backup to keep a copy of them too.

Does Inly work with my brand of aligners?

Yes. Inly works with any removable clear aligner or retainer. You enter your own number of trays and how many days to wear each one.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone and Apple Watch, and in a backup location you choose. We don’t have servers and never see your data. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.
