Who we are

Inly is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that helps people track how long they wear clear aligners and retainers. In this policy, “Inly”, “we” and “us” refer to the developer of the app. If you have any question about privacy, write to privacy@healus-ai.com.

What the app stores, and where

To work, Inly keeps the information you give it:

  • wear sessions — when your aligners came out, when they went back in, and the reason you chose (for example, a meal);
  • your treatment — trays, tray lengths, phases such as refinements, retainer settings and appointment dates;
  • your preferences — daily goal, reminders, language, appearance and the first name you entered, if any;
  • progress photos you take with the in-app camera.

This information is stored only on your iPhone and, if you use the Apple Watch app, on your Apple Watch. It is shared between Inly and its widgets and Live Activity on the same device. It is never sent to us or to any server we operate — we do not operate any.

Apple Watch

The Watch app exchanges your current status and recent sessions with your iPhone directly, using Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. This data travels between your own devices and is not sent to us.

Backups

If you turn on backups, Inly writes a backup file to a folder you select in the Files app — for example iCloud Drive. That file is handled by the storage provider you chose, under its own privacy terms (for iCloud, Apple’s Privacy Policy). We cannot access it. You can turn backups off and delete the file at any time.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, Inly keeps a compact copy of your wear history, trays, notes and settings in your own iCloud account, using Apple’s iCloud key-value storage, so it can appear on another iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID. Photos are not included. This copy is stored by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; it is never sent to us and we cannot read it. You can turn sync off at any time in Settings → Backup and data.

Purchases

Inly+ is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and tells the app on your device whether Inly+ is unlocked. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Camera and photos

Inly asks for camera access only so you can take progress photos. Photos are saved inside the app on your device. They are not uploaded anywhere, and they are only included in a report if you choose to add them when you export.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. Inly does not use push notification servers, so no notification content passes through us.

Reports you export

When you export a PDF report, it is created on your device. It leaves your device only if you share it yourself, for example with your orthodontist. What happens to it after that depends on how and with whom you share it.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or advertising services.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.
  • Inly does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics about App Store downloads and, if you have opted in on your device, crash reports. These come from Apple and do not identify you.

Health information

Wear time and treatment details can relate to your health. Because they stay on your devices and in storage you control, you remain in control of them at all times.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual sessions, trays and photos in the app. To delete everything, delete Inly from your iPhone and Apple Watch, and remove any backup file you created. Since we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Inly can be used by teenagers in orthodontic treatment, ideally with a parent or guardian. Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on Netlify; like any web host, Netlify may process your IP address in its server logs for security and reliability, as described in Netlify’s Privacy Policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, these requests can usually be met directly in the app. If you have any question, contact us and we will reply.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Inly handles data — for example, if a future version offers an optional sync feature — we will update this page and change the date above before the change takes effect.
